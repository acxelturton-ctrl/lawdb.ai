# Architecture

US law-firm directory. Everything is US only: 50 states + DC (`src/lib/us.js` is the single source for state names, slugs, time zones, map centres and the "is this point in the US" test).

## Pieces
- **Static site** (repo root): `index.html`, `find-lawyers.html`, case-type pages (`*-lawyers.html`, generated by `scripts/build-case-pages.mjs` from `data/case-types.json`), legal pages, `for-firms.html`, `badge.html`. CSS/JS are plain files; no framework.
- **Worker** (`src/worker.js`): routes `/api/*`, `/firms/<slug>` (rendered from D1), `/news`, sitemaps, state/city scoped lists. Files named in `assets.run_worker_first` in `wrangler.jsonc` go through the Worker; everything else is served straight from assets.
- **D1** (`DB`): schema in `migrations/0001_schema.sql`. Firms have a `state` (2-letter) column. Firm pages default to `page_mode = 'dynamic'`.
- **KV** (`BACKUPS`) and **R2** (`IMAGES`): backups and uploaded images. Bindings are commented in `wrangler.jsonc` until the resources exist.
- **Blog reader** (`src/lib/feeds.js`): follows each firm's blog feed and shows the latest posts on `/news` and the home slider.
- **Verification** (`src/lib/verification.js`): checks a lawyer against the state bar directory (`STATE_BARS`).

## Home page (`index.html`)
Order: hero (animated demo, search) → numbers → **Law firms to explore** → What do you need help with → Find lawyers in your state → articles slider → **More from the firms + Firm spotlight** → How it works → Why LawDB (LawDB AI, Verified, complete profiles, guides, free) → For law firms → Questions (with FAQPage JSON-LD). Styles: `home-gold.css` (hero, numbers, states, slider), `home-wide.css` (desktop width), `home-v6.css` (the content sections, all classes `hv-*`).
- The real-data sections are **filled in by the Worker** (`src/routes/home-showcase.js`, called from `dynamic.js`): they are `hidden` in the file and shown only when the database answers, so the page is never broken by it. Code: `src/render/home-showcase.js` (card, spotlight, article row).
- **Who can appear:** a live firm with its own logo, a cover picture, a phone, a practice area and a description of 90 words or more. Featured firms (at most 4, a different city each) lead and are labelled; the rest of the 12 cards are a daily pick (seeded by the Eastern date, so everyone sees the same row, tomorrow's differs) alternating between states, at most 2 per city and about 3 per practice area. The spotlight is one more firm (Featured, else Verified, else best look), never one of the cards. The Florida/Texas tabs filter the cards in the browser.
- **Chips are only what the data says:** Verified (`firm_verifications`), Free consultation, 24/7, Virtual (`perks` crawled from the firm's own site), Habla español (`details.languages`). Nothing is invented; the one illustration with made-up content (the chat example, the "Harbour & Finch" firm in For law firms) is labelled Example / Sample preview.
- The article list continues where the slider stops (`sliderPosts` in `home-news.js`); one article per firm.
- Memory: each server keeps the pick for 10 minutes (the page itself is cached 60 s); a failed query is not retried for a minute.
- Tests: `scripts/test-home-showcase.mjs` (part of `npm run check`).

## Sign-up and the way into LawDB Connect
`/account/?join=1` (sign-up) and `/account/` (sign-in) are one page (`renderLogin` in `account/account.js`): Google and LinkedIn buttons when configured, the email link when the server can send mail (`emailConfigured`, `GET /api/auth/providers`), otherwise an honest "opening soon" note. Setup steps: `docs/SIGN_IN_SETUP.md`.
- Path of a firm: sign-up (email link, one use, 15 minutes) -> four-screen welcome (name, city, role, goal) -> "Find or add my firm" -> claim -> **approved at once if the email is on the firm's own website domain** (`src/lib/email-match.js`, `autoApproves` in `routes/account.js`; otherwise an admin approves; `AUTO_APPROVE_CLAIMS=0` turns it off) -> My firm page with the LawDB Connect card -> free month (`/trial`) -> LawDB Connect tab.
- Links and OAuth redirects use `siteOrigin(env, url)` (`lib/auth.js`): the real site, or the `*.workers.dev` preview itself, so sign-in links work before the domain is live.
- Tests: `scripts/test-signup.mjs` (part of `npm run check`).

## LawDB AI (home page chat)
`ai-search.js` / `ai-search.css` draw a round AI icon at the bottom right of every public page (loaded from `site.js`); tapping it opens the chat in a panel. It posts to `POST /api/ai/match` (`src/routes/ai.js` -> `src/lib/ai-match.js`).
- The model (Cloudflare Workers AI, binding `AI`, `@cf/meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct-fp8-fast`) only **reads** the situation: case type, city, state, a neutral one-line summary. It never writes text for the visitor and gives no legal advice; every sentence shown is a template in `ai-match.js`.
- If the model is missing, slow or wrong, a keyword matcher gives the same fields, so the feature always answers. The model's city/state are accepted only if they match a city that has a live firm.
- Firms come from D1 (live firms of the case type in the state, ranked by distance, Featured firms a few miles closer). Spanish-speaking filter uses `details.languages`.
- Nothing the visitor types is stored. Throttles: 30 requests per visitor per hour and `AI_DAILY_CAP` (default 3000) model calls a day, counted in `stats_daily`; past the cap the keyword matcher answers.
- Filters: Spanish-speaking (`details.languages`), Free consultation and Available 24/7 (`perks` in the firm record: what the firm's own website says, from the crawl, shown as chips on the profile and as pills in the chat). A filter nobody satisfies is relaxed and the reply says so. Verified firms (a row in `firm_verifications`) rank 12 miles closer and show a Verified pill. The chat is free for every visitor and every firm; Featured firms are simply listed first when they are close (same city or within 40 miles, or anywhere in the state when the whole state is asked for), and the chat's note says so.
- Bilingual, by choice: the chat opens with a language choice (English / Español) and the EN/ES button stays in its header. It answers only in the language the visitor chose (`T.en` / `T.es` in `ai-match.js`, `UI` in `ai-search.js`), never one it guessed; it understands Spanish or English either way. If Spanish is typed while English is chosen, the answer stays English and carries `offerLang` so the chat shows a "Cambiar a español" button. The one-line summary passed to the firm stays in English, with a note that the visitor writes Spanish.
- "Request consultation" opens the firm page with `?ask=<summary>#contact`, which pre-fills the (editable) message.
- Tests: `scripts/test-ai-match.mjs` (part of `npm run check`).

## LawDB Connect (Featured plan, $89/month)
A members-only directory where Featured firms find and connect with firms in other cities and states. Code: `src/lib/network.js` (logic), `src/routes/network.js` (API under `/api/account/firms/:slug/network/...` and `/api/admin/network/...`), `account/network.js` + `network.css` (the "Network" tab in the firm dashboard), `admin/admin.js` (the "Network" admin tab: reports, remove/restore). Tables: `network_profiles`, `network_connections`, `network_blocks`, `network_reports` (`migrations/0002_network.sql`).
- **Who is listed:** a firm that is live, Featured (paid or the free month), has switched itself on and accepted the rules, and is not suspended. A firm that stops being Featured leaves the directory by itself (the queries join `firms.featured`). A Basic firm sees a locked preview made of clearly labelled sample firms (`account/network-sample.js`) and a button to start the free month.
- **What is shown:** what LawDB verified (`firm_verifications`) is kept apart from what the firm reports about itself. "Accepting referrals" appears only if the firm set it, with the date.
- **Search:** structured filters (practice area, state, city, open to, language, kind of firm, accepting referrals, verified) plus a plain-language bar. The AI (Workers AI, same model and daily cap as the public chat) only turns the sentence into filters; if it is missing or wrong a keyword reader does it. "Why this matches" is built only from filters that matched and never says anything about skill.
- **Connections:** request with a note (300 characters, no markup), the other firm accepts or declines. Limits: 10 requests a day and 25 waiting per firm, no re-asking for 30 days after a decline. Accepting shares the two contact people's names and emails; nothing is shared before. Either side can end a connection. Blocking hides both firms from each other and ends the connection. Both firms must still be listed to accept. Requests and acceptances send an email.
- **Trust and safety:** firms accept the rules (`RULES` in `network.js`, version recorded) before listing; reports go to the admin tab and by email; the admin can remove a firm (its requests and connections end and it cannot list itself again) and restore it. All actions are in the audit log.
- **No fees:** LawDB charges and takes nothing on referrals. Referral and fee-sharing rules differ by state; a US attorney must review this feature before launch.
- **Not built yet:** posting referral opportunities, tracking referrals, messaging, AI referral drafting, a network map. Tests: `scripts/test-network.mjs` (real SQLite, part of `npm run check`).

## Data pipeline (`tools/us-data`)
1. `pull_overture.py` reads US law-firm places from Overture Maps (open data) and filters them to the target state.
2. `enrich.py` visits each firm's own website (robots.txt respected, 1 request/second/site) for logo, photos, about text, socials, blog feed and email.
3. `build_import_sql.py` writes `data/states/<ST>/import.sql` (INSERT OR IGNORE) to load into D1.

Rules that keep it safe to scale: a firm needs a website and a US address to be imported; pages with no real description stay `noindex` until they have one (thin-page guard); states are rolled out in waves, and the sitemaps only list indexable pages.

## Deploy
Pushes to `main` build and deploy through Cloudflare Workers Builds. Until lawdb.ai is connected the site is on its `workers.dev` address and is marked `noindex` (see `_headers`).
