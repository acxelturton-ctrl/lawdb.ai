# Architecture

US law-firm directory. Everything is US only: 50 states + DC (`src/lib/us.js` is the single source for state names, slugs, time zones, map centres and the "is this point in the US" test).

## Pieces
- **Static site** (repo root): `index.html`, `find-lawyers.html`, case-type pages (`*-lawyers.html`, generated by `scripts/build-case-pages.mjs` from `data/case-types.json`), legal pages, `for-firms.html`, `badge.html`. CSS/JS are plain files; no framework.
- **Worker** (`src/worker.js`): routes `/api/*`, `/firms/<slug>` (rendered from D1), `/news`, sitemaps, state/city scoped lists. Files named in `assets.run_worker_first` in `wrangler.jsonc` go through the Worker; everything else is served straight from assets.
- **D1** (`DB`): schema in `migrations/0001_schema.sql`. Firms have a `state` (2-letter) column. Firm pages default to `page_mode = 'dynamic'`.
- **KV** (`BACKUPS`) and **R2** (`IMAGES`): backups and uploaded images. Bindings are commented in `wrangler.jsonc` until the resources exist.
- **Blog reader** (`src/lib/feeds.js`): follows each firm's blog feed and shows the latest posts on `/news` and the home slider.
- **Verification** (`src/lib/verification.js`): checks a lawyer against the state bar directory (`STATE_BARS`).

## Data pipeline (`tools/us-data`)
1. `pull_overture.py` reads US law-firm places from Overture Maps (open data) and filters them to the target state.
2. `enrich.py` visits each firm's own website (robots.txt respected, 1 request/second/site) for logo, photos, about text, socials, blog feed and email.
3. `build_import_sql.py` writes `data/states/<ST>/import.sql` (INSERT OR IGNORE) to load into D1.

Rules that keep it safe to scale: a firm needs a website and a US address to be imported; pages with no real description stay `noindex` until they have one (thin-page guard); states are rolled out in waves, and the sitemaps only list indexable pages.

## Deploy
Pushes to `main` build and deploy through Cloudflare Workers Builds. Until lawdb.ai is connected the site is on its `workers.dev` address and is marked `noindex` (see `_headers`).
