    Good to know

    Questions, answered

    The short version of how LawDB works. Still unsure? Ask us.

    Contact us
    Is LawDB free to use?

    Yes. Searching, comparing firms, reading the guides and using LawDB AI are free, and you do not need an account. Signing in, which is also free, lets you follow firms and save stories.

    How does LawDB choose the firms it shows?

    Firms are listed from public business information and each firm's own website. A firm appears on LawDB once it has a complete profile: a logo, a cover photo and a written description of its practice. Featured firms are a paid plan and are always labelled Featured.

    What does “Verified by LawDB” mean?

    We check that a lawyer at the firm is in the state bar's public directory, that the person managing the profile is who they say they are, and that the office details match. It is not an endorsement and it does not say anything about a lawyer's record, so check the state bar's own directory too. Each profile links to it.

    Does LawDB give legal advice or recommend a lawyer?

    No. LawDB is an independent online directory. We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice. A listing is not a recommendation, and LawDB AI helps you find firms that fit what you describe; it cannot tell you what to do about your case.

    Which states do you cover?

    Florida and Texas today, with California, New York, Illinois and the rest of the country coming next.

    How do I contact a lawyer?

    Open a firm's profile to call, visit its website or get directions. You deal with the firm directly. Some firms also offer a booking button on their profile.

    How do I list my firm, or fix my listing?

    List your firm free to add your photos, description and practice areas. If your firm is already here, open its page and choose “Own this firm?”, or contact us and we will correct or remove it.
