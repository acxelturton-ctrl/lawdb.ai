Questions firms ask

Is LawDB Marketplace free to join?

Yes. Joining and browsing are free for every firm. You pay only when you unlock an inquiry, and the price is shown before you do.

Is an inquiry shared with more than one firm?

No. Each inquiry is exclusive. The first verified firm to unlock it receives the contact details, and the inquiry disappears for every other firm.

What do I see before I unlock?

The practice area, city and state, how urgent it is, how long ago it came in and a one-line summary. The summary never names the person. Their name, email and phone number stay hidden until you unlock.

Who can unlock inquiries?

Firms verified by LawDB. At launch you send your state bar number or a link to your listing and we confirm it by hand.

Is the price tied to whether the person hires me?

No. The price is a flat advertising fee for the inquiry. It does not depend on whether the person becomes your client, and LawDB takes no share of any fee.
